01 · About

Consistent publishing,
without the backend mess.

I am a content operations specialist based in Koronadal City, Philippines, working remotely with YouTube creators, coaches and personal brands around the world. I spend my days inside content calendars, YouTube Studio, Asana boards and publishing workflows, the parts of the business most creators do not want to touch.

My style is simple: clear systems, steady execution, no surprises. I use AI tools where they save real time, adapt to whatever stack you already run on, and treat your content backend like it is my own channel. If it is a recurring task, I turn it into a checklist so it stops eating your week.

02 · Services

What I handle
behind the scenes.

  • 01

    Content calendar management

    Weekly and monthly content plans, themes organised, post formats aligned and deliverables kept on track. You always know what is going out, when, and what is still missing.

  • 02

    YouTube publishing support

    Uploads, descriptions, tags, playlists, end screens, visibility settings and scheduled publishing handled inside YouTube Studio. The video is ready to go before you have to look at it.

  • 03

    Titles, thumbnails and packaging

    Title ideation, A/B testing workflows, thumbnail direction and coordination, and metadata packaging for stronger positioning. Five thumbnails across five niches are in the work section below.

  • 04

    Shorts, clipping and repurposing

    Long videos clipped into Shorts and Reels, packaged and posted on a steady cadence, with the same asset reused across YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest and LinkedIn. One recording, a week of posts.

  • 05

    Video editing and social design

    Long form narrative edits, explainers, vlogs, product videos and platform native social edits, plus branded post designs and carousels. Seven edited samples and ten designs are below.

  • 06

    SOPs and content systems

    Repeatable workflows written down, release checklists, Asana and inbox structure, and repurposing plans your team can actually follow. Built where the client had no organised process at all.
